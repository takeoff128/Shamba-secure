# Kenya livestock market prices (compiled October 2026)

## How to use these prices
- These are indicative reference prices compiled on 25 October 2026 from the sources listed. Prices change weekly and differ by county, season (higher around December and Eid), size and buyer.
- For today's price, the assistant searches the web when live search is available; otherwise quote these with their date and source and advise checking with local buyers.
- The farm's own sale prices recorded in Shamba Secure are often the best guide for that farm.

## Milk prices
- Farmgate milk: President Ruto announced KES 65 per litre plus a KES 2 per litre quarterly bonus, from October 2026, up from KES 60 (Dairy Business MEA, 21 Oct 2026). Announced by the President on Mashujaa Day (20 Oct 2026); processors and brokers may pay different rates.
- Milk supply was tight in mid-2026: formal deliveries fell from 84.4 million litres (June) to 81.3 million (July) (Kenya Dairy Board via Daily Nation, Sept 2026).

## Egg prices
- Retail, 30-egg tray, Nairobi and outskirts, about September 2026: KES 504–720, up from KES 450–550 (Daily Nation).
- Neighbourhood shops: about KES 470–510 per tray (Githurai 45 and Nyeri); 3 eggs for KES 50.
- Supermarkets: about KES 504–604 per tray for table eggs; about KES 720 for free-range.
- Causes cited: cold weather cutting laying rates, high feed costs and poor feed quality.
- Farmgate prices are lower than retail; expect less than shop prices when selling to traders.

## Chicken prices
- Broilers, live weight (reported Feb–Mar 2026): KES 276–600 per kg, median about KES 350/kg; e.g. Githurai about 342, Isinya about 350, Limuru about 325–350, Naromoru about 276, Wakulima (Nairobi) 500 (Agrisoko254 market intelligence: farmer/trader reports and baseline data; KAMIS for some markets). The KES 350 median includes a KES 600 Mukuyu report from 7 Feb. Indicative only.
- Kienyeji chicken is usually sold per bird and fetches more than broilers; check local prices.

## Day-old chick prices
Indicative list (Beyond Forest, updated September 2026):
- Broiler chicks: about KES 95–105 (Kenchic, Irvines, Abbas, Suguna about 105; Isinya, Silverlands about 95).
- Layer chicks: about KES 130–160 (Kenchic about 150–160).
- Improved kienyeji chicks: about KES 110–130 (Kenbro about 115, Kuroiler about 110, Sasso about 130, Navogen kienyeji cross about 120).
- Kuroiler growers: about KES 190 (2 weeks), 250 (3 weeks), 300 (4 weeks), 450 (6 weeks).

## Goat, sheep and cattle prices
- Goats/sheep, live, Kiamaiko market Nairobi (March 2026): small about KES 9,000–10,000; medium about KES 12,000–14,000; large about KES 15,000–18,000+. Goats overall about KES 10,000–16,000+, sheep about KES 8,000–13,000 (Beyond Forest).
- Live goat estimate (June 2026): farmgate about KES 280/kg, retail about KES 480/kg (Selina Wamucii aggregated estimate; low confidence).
- Dairy cattle (2026 price guide, Beyond Forest; dealer listings, low confidence): calves 0–2 months KES 25,000–35,000; weaners 3–6 months KES 45,000–60,000; bulling heifers KES 80,000–130,000; in-calf heifers about KES 140,000–260,000 depending on stage and grade; lactating cows about KES 180,000 (20–25 L/day) to KES 260,000–290,000 (45+ L/day).
- Beef and local cattle prices vary widely by region, body condition and drought; check the nearest livestock market or county livestock office.

## Pig and pork prices
- Pork retail estimate: about KES 450 per kg (June 2026, Selina Wamucii aggregated estimate; low confidence). Live pig prices are usually quoted per kg live or dead weight by buyers such as processors and butcheries; confirm with the buyer before selling.

## Feed prices
Indicative (Fama, Sept 2026; prices vary widely by brand and region, low confidence):
- Chick mash 70 kg: about KES 3,300. Growers mash 70 kg: about KES 4,800.
- Layers mash 70 kg: about KES 3,150–5,000. Layers mash 50 kg: about KES 3,900–4,200.
- Dairy meal 70 kg: about KES 2,300–3,400.
- Pig feeds 70 kg: about KES 2,400–3,400.
- Compare feeds by price per kg and by quality; poor-quality feed costs more in slow growth.
