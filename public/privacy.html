What information we collect

  • Account information: name, phone number, and email address, used to log in and to recover a forgotten password.
  • Farm records you enter: transactions, debts (including names, amounts, and optionally phone numbers of people who owe you or are owed), livestock records, and broiler lot data.
  • Reminder recipients: if you add a phone number to a debt, that number is used solely to send a payment reminder SMS on the due date.

What we don't collect

This app does not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any device data beyond what's needed to keep you logged in. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics/tracking.

How your information is used

  • To operate your farm's records within the app.
  • To send SMS reminders (via Africa's Talking) — to your own farm's users, or to a debtor's phone number only when you've provided one for money owed to you.
  • To email a password reset link when requested, to the email address on file for that account only.
